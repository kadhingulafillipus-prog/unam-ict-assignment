## 1. Introduction and Learning Objectives
Welcome to this educational learning website designed to support Grade 10 English as a Second Language (ESL) learners in developing narrative writing skills. This resource guides learners through understanding how stories are planned, structured, and executed.

### Measurable Learning Objectives
By the end of this digital unit, learners will be able to:
1. Identify and define at least five core elements of a narrative (Setting, Characters, Plot, Conflict, Climax, and Resolution).
2. Organize story milestones chronologically into a clear beginning, middle, and ending framework.
3. Apply structural linking words, descriptive sensory language, and varied sentence types to write a coherent narrative of 200–250 words.

---

## 2. Learning Content & Teaching Aids
A narrative is a story that presents a sequence of events in a logical order, which can be based on real-life or imagined experiences.

### Key Story Elements
* **Setting:** Where and when the story happens (time, weather, atmosphere).
* **Characters:** The figures involved in the events.
* **Plot Structure:** Moving from **Exposition** (introduction), through **Rising Action** (building the problem), to the **Climax** (intense turning point), and concluding with the **Resolution** (ending).
* **Language Tools:** Incorporate descriptive language (sensory details like sound and sight) and natural transitions (e.g., *afterwards, suddenly, meanwhile*).

## 3. ICT-Integrated Lesson Plan (60 Minutes)
* **Introduction (5 min):** Stimulate prior knowledge by discussing what makes a story interesting.
* **Core Teaching (15 min):** Explain the 5 narrative elements and paragraphing structures using the website content.
* **Guided Practice (15 min):** Learners work through the digital interactive story planner template to outline their thoughts.
* **Assessment (10 min):** Learners complete the online 5-question quiz to receive instant comprehension feedback.
* **Independent Work (10 min):** Learners draft their final 200–250 word narrative story.
* **Closure (5 min):** Learners share one specific writing improvement goal for their drafts.

### Pedagogical Approach
This website utilizes an activity-based, **learner-centered constructivist approach**. Instead of passive copying, the digital architecture moves learners through independent scaffolding—starting with concept absorption, moving to interactive digital planning, and ending with real-time assessment.
