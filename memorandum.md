# Teacher Memorandum / Answer Key
**Subject:** English as a Second Language (ESL) | **Grade:** 10 | **Topic:** Narrative Writing

#### Section A: Multiple-Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. **Which part of a narrative normally introduces the setting and characters?**
   * **Correct Answer:** C. Exposition
2. **Which stage contains the most intense or important moment?**
   * **Correct Answer:** B. Climax
3. **Which word best shows that one event happens after another?**
   * **Correct Answer:** C. Afterwards
4. **Which sentence uses descriptive language most effectively?**
   * **Correct Answer:** C. "The icy wind whispered through the broken window."

#### Section B: Short Answer Questions
5. **Name any two important elements of a narrative. (2 Marks)**
   * *Expected Answers:* Setting, Characters, Plot, Conflict, Climax, or Resolution. (1 mark per element).
6. **What is the conflict in a story? (2 Marks)**
   * *Expected Answer:** The conflict is the main problem or struggle that creates tension in the story.
7. **Put these stages in the correct order: Resolution, Climax, Exposition, Rising action. (2 Marks)**
   * *Correct Order:* Exposition -> Rising action -> Climax -> Resolution.
